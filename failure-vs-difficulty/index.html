Failure rate vs difficulty

One curve per model — a thinking variant counts as its own model; benchmarks pooled. The Dataset switch picks the tasks used (wave 1+2 first), the Models switch below the chart picks the models.

Definitions
  • Failure rate — a model's share of failed attempts on a task; each curve is one model's failure rate against task difficulty, benchmarks pooled; a thinking variant counts as its own model.
  • Task difficulty — a task's position on the shared difficulty axis, the axis the outcome-distributions page shares, in logit units unless the X scale reads Raw.
  • Average rate / Median task — the definition switch picks one estimator: the average failure rate over the tasks, or the median task's failure rate; the trend, the bands and the crossings follow it.
  • D50 — the difficulty at which the curve first crosses a 50% failure rate upward.
  • D99 — the difficulty at which the curve first crosses 1%, a 99% solve chance.
  • Band — the posterior interval of the fitted curve at the chosen level (80% or 90%; 95% where available); the bracket beside a crossing is where the band's edges cross the same level.
  • Curve estimator — Bayesian: the served Gaussian-task-effect posterior, its median curve drawn, a model without a posterior not drawn; Local-logistic fit: the descriptive fit, the curve and its band carry the measurement.
