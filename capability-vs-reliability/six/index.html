About this view

Each panel is the main page’s plane for one set: D99 (vertical) against D50 (horizontal) on that set’s own difficulty axis, equal logit scales and the y = x line. A model’s crossing beyond the set’s easiest or hardest task is an open mark at the edge pointing the way it lies. A model is a point in a panel only where it has a fit on that set; the count in a panel’s corner is the number of points drawn. The sets shown are chosen above the figure; the level controls, the definition and estimator switches, the chips and the export are the main page’s, applied to every panel at once.
